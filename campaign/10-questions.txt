================================================================================

[Q10.1] The Samsung S5K4H8 (Rear) and Hynix HI551 (Front) camera sensors share `I2C1` with the
        AW9523B keyboard controller and AW9120 LED driver.
        - What happens to keyboard scan latency when the camera driver performs large multi-byte
          I2C register burst writes during active video capture?
        - Does the I2C bus arbiter enforce fair queuing?

        sensors.
        - What is the mandatory power stabilization delay between enabling AVDD/DVDD and
          releasing sensor reset (`GPIO32`/`GPIO33`) to prevent sensor I2C state machine lockup?

[Q10.3] How does the SENINF0..7 CSI-2 receiver handle virtual channel multiplexing when both
        front and rear cameras are probed simultaneously?
