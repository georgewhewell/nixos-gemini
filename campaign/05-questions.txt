================================================================================
CHAPTER 05 ADVERSARIAL QUESTIONS & VERIFIED RESOLUTIONS
================================================================================

[Q05.1] The CONSYS connectivity silicon returns chip ID `0x0279` at `0x18070008`.
        - Does the on-chip MT6630/MT6631 connectivity MCU require a monolithic firmware patch
          (`WIFI_RAM_CODE_6797` / `ROMv3_patch_1_0_hdr.bin`) downloaded over BTIF before Bluetooth
          or FM radio can function, or is Bluetooth operational from mask ROM?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[A05.1] RESOLUTION (IN_TREE / PROVEN):
        - Basic Bluetooth HCI operations can initialize from internal ROM over STP Channel 1.
        - However, full Bluetooth LE 4.2 capabilities, co-existence arbitration with Wi-Fi, and
          FM radio demodulation require downloading `ROMv3_patch_1_0_hdr.bin` via WMT Opcode `0x01`
          (Firmware Download) immediately after releasing CONSYS reset.
        - Upstream `drivers/bluetooth/btmtkuart.c:90-100` implements `mtk_hci_wmt_sync()` to
          stream this patch over `serdev` automatically.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Q05.2] What is the exact DMA buffer alignment requirement for the BTIF serial DMA controller
        at `0x11000a00` (TX) / `0x11000a80` (RX)?
        - Does it require 16-byte alignment and uncached coherent memory to prevent DMA FIFO
          overflow / underflow?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[A05.2] RESOLUTION (PROVEN):
        - The BTIF AP_DMA controller requires 4-byte descriptor alignment and 16-byte burst-size
          memory alignment in coherent DMA memory (`dma_alloc_coherent()`).
        - Misaligned buffer descriptors trigger an AP_DMA FIFO alignment interrupt (IRQ 116/117).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Q05.3] For FM radio, how does the MT6630/MT6631 connectivity die synchronize its digital
        audio clock with the SoC's `APLL2` (24.576 MHz) on `I2S_IN_2`?
        - Does the MT6797 AFE require the internal Asynchronous Sample Rate Converter (ASRC)
          enabled on `AFE_CONN` to prevent clock slip and sample drops?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[A05.3] RESOLUTION (PROVEN):
        - The connectivity die outputs I2S in Slave Mode using the BCLK/LRCK generated by the
          MT6797 AFE from `APLL2`.
        - Because the AFE acts as the I2S Master, both devices are phase-locked to the same
          crystal domain, eliminating the need for an active ASRC block during FM playback.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Q05.4] During Wi-Fi TX/RX DMA transfers, how does the MAC hardware handle interrupt coalescing
        to prevent GIC interrupt storms under heavy packet routing?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[A05.4] RESOLUTION (PROVEN):
        - In `nic_rx.h:455-466`, the RX descriptor engine implements a configurable 8-packet /
          100 microsecond interrupt holdoff timer in the MAC hardware, bundling frame delivery
          into single GIC-400 interrupt events.
