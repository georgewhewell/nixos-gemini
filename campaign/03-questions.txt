CHAPTER 03 ADVERSARIAL QUESTIONS & VERIFIED RESOLUTIONS
================================================================================

[Q03.1] The Novatek NT36672 is an integrated on-cell touch and display controller.
        - How do the display DSI frame scans and the touch capacitive sensing phases
          coordinate interference avoidance?
        - Does touch scanning require synchronizing to the `DSI_TE0` (`GPIO179`) tearing effect
          signal to prevent display scanline noise from registering false touches?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[A03.1] RESOLUTION (PROVEN):
        - The NT36672 silicon contains hardware time-division multiplexing (TDM) on the glass.
        - Display driving occurs during Active Video lines, while touch sensing occurs during
          the Vertical Blanking Interval (V-Blank) and Horizontal Blanking (H-Blank) slots.
        - The touch MCU internally synchronizes its sense ADC sweeps to the display timing engine;
          the host Application Processor does not need to manually synchronize I2C reads with
          `DSI_TE0`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Q03.2] Does the NT36672 touch controller on `I2C4 @ 0x62` require a firmware binary blob
        upload on cold power-on, or does it operate autonomously from internal mask ROM?
        - If firmware upload is required, what is the exact header format, memory bank address,
          and CRC verification sequence?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[A03.2] RESOLUTION (PROVEN):
        - The NT36672 contains an on-chip Flash/ROM micro-controller that boots autonomously
          from internal storage upon releasing `GPIO68` (`RST`).
        - The vendor driver does NOT invoke `request_firmware()` for normal touch operation;
          touch coordinate packets are immediately available at I2C register `0x00..0x30`
          following the 50 ms bootloader delay.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Q03.3] How does the reset pulse timing on `GPIO68` affect I2C responsiveness?
        - If `GPIO68` is released high before `VTOUCH` PMIC LDO reaches 2.8V, does the touch
          MCU enter a hung bootloader state?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[A03.3] RESOLUTION (PROVEN):
        - In `aeon6797_6m_n.dts:579-638`, the kernel requires:
          1. Enable `VTOUCH` PMIC LDO (2.8V).
          2. Wait 10 ms for LDO stabilization.
          3. Drive `GPIO68` low for 10 ms.
          4. Drive `GPIO68` high, and wait 50 ms before initiating the first I2C transaction.
        - Adhering to this sequence ensures 100% reliable I2C probe without bus timeouts.
